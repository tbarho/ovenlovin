Weeknight Tomato Pasta

A simple pantry pasta for a weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz dried pasta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 handful basil leaves

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until just shy of al dente.
  2. Warm the oil and garlic, then add tomatoes, salt, and pepper.
  3. Toss the pasta with the sauce and tear in the basil.
