Cookbook:Pancakes

A simple skillet pancake batter.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Procedure

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients together.
  2. Stir in the milk, egg, and butter until just combined.
  3. Cook spoonfuls on a hot buttered skillet until golden on both sides.
