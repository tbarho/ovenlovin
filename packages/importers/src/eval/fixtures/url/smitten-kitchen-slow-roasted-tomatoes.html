slow-roasted tomatoes

If you’ve never made slow-roasted tomatoes before, prepare to have your mind blown because they’ve got very little to do with the packaged sun-dried variety: they’re flavorful, dry on the outside with some juiciness left within.

And what to do with them? Namely, anything. Or mix them with white beans or slivers of basil.

  • Cherry, grape or small Roma tomatoes
  • Whole cloves of garlic, unpeeled
  • Olive oil
  • Herbs, if you are using them
  • Salt and pepper

Preheat oven to 225°F. Halve each cherry or grape tomato crosswise, or Roma tomato lengthwise and arrange on a parchment-lined baking sheet along with the cloves of garlic. Drizzle with olive oil, just enough to make the tomatoes glisten. Sprinkle herbs on, if you are using them, and salt and pepper.

Bake the tomatoes in the oven for about 3 hours. You want the tomatoes to be shriveled and dry, but with a little juice left inside - this could take more or less time depending on the size of your tomatoes.

Either use them right away or let them cool, cover them with some extra olive oil and keep them in the fridge for the best summer condiment, ever.
