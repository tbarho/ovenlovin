One-Bowl Cocoa Snack Bars

Makes 16 bars. Stir, bake, and slice.

1/2 cup melted butter, 1 cup brown sugar, 2 eggs, 1 tsp vanilla, 3/4 cup cocoa powder, 1/2 cup all-purpose flour, 1/2 tsp salt, 1 cup chocolate chips.

Heat the oven to 350°F and line an 8-inch pan. Whisk the melted butter and brown sugar, then beat in the eggs and vanilla. Fold in cocoa, flour, and salt, then the chocolate chips. Spread in the pan and bake 22 to 25 minutes, until the center is just set. Cool, then cut into 16 squares.
