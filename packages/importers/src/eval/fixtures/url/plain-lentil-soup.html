Red Lentil Soup

Serves 4. A cumin-scented pot of red lentils.

2 tbsp olive oil · 1 onion, chopped · 2 garlic cloves, minced · 1 tsp cumin

1 cup red lentils · 4 cups vegetable broth · 1 lemon, juiced · 1 tsp salt

Cook the onion and garlic in olive oil until soft.

Stir in cumin, then the lentils and broth.

Simmer until the lentils fall apart, 15 to 20 minutes.

Add lemon juice and salt, then mash lightly.
