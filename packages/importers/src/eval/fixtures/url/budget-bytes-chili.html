Weeknight Chili

Serves 6. A pot of chili with beans and ground beef.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef ($3.20)
  • 1 onion, chopped ($0.40)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced ($0.16)
  • 2 tbsp chili powder ($0.20)
  • 1 tsp cumin ($0.08)
  • 1 can diced tomatoes ($0.70)
  • 1 can kidney beans, drained ($0.89)
  • 1 cup beef broth ($0.26)
  • 1 tsp salt ($0.01)

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic.
  2. Stir in chili powder and cumin.
  3. Add tomatoes, beans, broth, and salt.
  4. Simmer 30 minutes, stirring now and then.
