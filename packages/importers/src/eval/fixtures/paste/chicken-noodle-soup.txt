Chicken Noodle Soup

Weeknight soup with shredded chicken and egg noodles.

Serves 6
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 30 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
- 2 cups egg noodles
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Instructions:
1. Warm the oil and cook onion, carrot, and celery until soft.
2. Add broth and bring to a simmer.
3. Stir in chicken and noodles; cook until the noodles are tender.
4. Season with salt and pepper and finish with parsley.
