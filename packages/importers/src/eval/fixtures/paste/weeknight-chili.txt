Weeknight Chili

A pot of chili with beans and ground beef.

Serves 6
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 40 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can kidney beans, drained
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 tsp salt

Instructions:
1. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic.
2. Stir in chili powder and cumin.
3. Add tomatoes, beans, broth, and salt.
4. Simmer 30 minutes, stirring now and then.
