Chewy Oatmeal Cookies

Bakery-style oats with raisins.

Makes 24 cookies
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 12 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups old-fashioned oats
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3/4 cup raisins

Instructions:
1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Whisk oats, flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
3. Beat butter and brown sugar, then add the egg and vanilla.
4. Stir in the dry mix and raisins.
5. Scoop onto a sheet and bake 10 to 12 minutes.
