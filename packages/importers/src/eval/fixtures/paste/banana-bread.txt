Banana Bread

A one-bowl loaf for ripe bananas.

Serves 10
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 55 minutes

Ingredients:
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
2. Mash the bananas and stir in the melted butter.
3. Mix in sugar, egg, and vanilla.
4. Sprinkle baking soda and salt over the batter, then fold in the flour.
5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
