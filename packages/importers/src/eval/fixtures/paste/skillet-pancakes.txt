Skillet Pancakes

A small-batch pancake batter for a Saturday skillet.

Serves 4
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 15 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions:
1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and baking powder together.
2. Stir in the milk, egg, and melted butter until just combined.
3. Cook spoonfuls on a hot buttered skillet until golden on both sides.
