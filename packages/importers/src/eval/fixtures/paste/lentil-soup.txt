Red Lentil Soup

A cumin-scented pot of red lentils.

Serves 4
Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 25 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 cup red lentils
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tsp salt

Instructions:
1. Cook the onion and garlic in olive oil until soft.
2. Stir in cumin, then the lentils and broth.
3. Simmer until the lentils fall apart, 15 to 20 minutes.
4. Add lemon juice and salt, then mash lightly.
