Chicken Pot Pie

Makes 2 potpies (8 servings each)
Prep time: 25 minutes
Cook time: 40 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 refrigerated pie crusts
- 2 cups diced cooked chicken
- 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
- 1/3 cup butter
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 3/4 cups chicken broth
- 2/3 cup milk

Instructions:
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Melt butter, whisk in flour, salt, and pepper, then the broth and milk until thick.
3. Stir in chicken and vegetables.
4. Divide the filling between two crust-lined pie plates and cover with the top crusts.
5. Bake 30 to 35 minutes, until the crusts are golden.
